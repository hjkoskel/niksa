Signal Relay
Peer-to-peer video and serial-port bridge over WebRTC. No server — connections are paired by copy/pasting a short connection code between devices.
Source

Camera

Captures local video and a local serial port. Streams video out, forwards serial bytes over the link, writes incoming link bytes back to the port.

Remote

Viewer

Watches the incoming video feed and exchanges text/command messages with the camera side's serial port.

Web Serial requires Chrome or Edge. Camera and serial access require a secure context (https://, localhost, or a local file).

Camera

Serial port

Bytes read from the port are forwarded over the data channel as-is (binary safe). Bytes arriving over the data channel are written straight back to the port. WebUSB fallback supports CH340/CH341 and FTDI adapters at 2400–115200 baud, for browsers (e.g. Android Chrome) where Web Serial can't see USB-serial hardware yet.

Sensors

Uses this device's Geolocation and motion sensors, when present, and sends CRSF 0x02 GPS / 0x13 Accel-Gyro frames over the same data channel as the serial bytes. Axis signs are best-effort and depend on how this device is mounted on the vehicle.
